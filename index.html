Meg Alivien is a writer, cat-mom, and wife.

Other than writing, you can find Meg listening to audiobooks, binging her favorite tv shows, learning new recipes, and fangirling over her favorite fictional characters.

Meg is the author of cozy and spicy paranormal romance. Her small town Monster Boyfriends series, described as Halloweentown meets Gilmore Girls, has over 3 million page reads on Amazon and can be found in indie bookstores nationwide!
