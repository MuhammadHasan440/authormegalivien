🌶️ Spice Rating Scale

This is how I rate the spice in my books on a scale of 1 to 5 — 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest! Find your perfect heat level and dive into the coziest (or spiciest) reads.

🦇 Werewolves, Vampires, and Ghosts... oh my! ✦ Paranormal fantasy & romance by Meg Alivien ✦ Cozy reads with a little spice! 🍑

The Heat Scale

From sweet and cozy to fiery and intense — here's what each pepper level means so you can pick the perfect read.

🌶️
Beginner
Very mild. Sweet, cozy, and entirely fade-to-black. Perfect for readers who want romance without heat.
1 Pepper
🌶️ 🌶️
Easy
Warm with a little spice. Light intimacy and tension, but still on the softer side of steamy.
2 Peppers
🌶️ 🌶️ 🌶️

Hot and clearly on-page. Moderate heat with balanced story-to-spice ratio. A solid middle ground.
3 Peppers

Maximum heat. Very explicit, frequent, and intense scenes. Not for the faint of heart — bring water.
5 Peppers
